# Harbor City Maritime Museum: Visitor Information

The Harbor City Maritime Museum opened in 1987 in the restored East Pier warehouse. The
museum is open Tuesday through Sunday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. It is closed on Mondays.

Adult admission is $14, admission for students and seniors is $9, and children under six
enter free. The first Sunday of each month is free for all visitors. Advance reservations
are recommended for groups of ten or more, but individual visitors do not need a reservation.

The museum's permanent collection includes 1,200 catalogued objects. The lighthouse gallery
is on the second floor and is accessible by elevator. Guided tours begin at 11 a.m. and 2 p.m.
on Saturdays. The museum address is 18 East Pier, Harbor City.
