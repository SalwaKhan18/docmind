# Northstar Solar Co-op: Member Guide

Northstar Solar Co-op began operating in 2021 in Madison, Wisconsin. Its community solar
garden has 240 panels and a rated capacity of 96 kilowatts. Members receive monthly bill
credits based on their share of the garden's electricity production.

The standard membership share costs $18 per month. There is no enrollment fee, and members
may cancel with 30 days' notice. Bill credits vary with weather and household allocation;
the co-op does not guarantee a fixed monthly dollar saving.

The garden generated 128,400 kilowatt-hours in 2025. The co-op publishes an annual production
summary each January. Members can contact the member desk at members@northstarsolar.example
or visit the Madison office on weekdays from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
