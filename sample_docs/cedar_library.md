# Cedar County Library: Digital Lending Policy

Cedar County Library cardholders may borrow up to five digital books at a time. A digital
loan lasts 21 days and returns automatically at the end of the loan period. Readers may
return a title early from the library app.

The library added digital lending in 2019. New cardholders can apply online or at any branch;
proof of Cedar County residence is required. There is no charge for a standard library card.

The digital collection is refreshed on the first Wednesday of each month. If every copy of a
title is in use, a cardholder may place a hold. The library sends an email when a held title
becomes available, and the reader has 72 hours to borrow it.
